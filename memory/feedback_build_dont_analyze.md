Across the YE session (2026-08-17) Oussama asked for an app four times before getting one. Each time I returned analysis instead: a reconciliation plan, a spec, an engine with no UI, a terminal report. He also twice had to reject urgency I invented ("the Fall window is closing, act now") when he had never framed the work as time-critical.

His correction, verbatim in spirit: *"nothing is urgent as of now — we don't depend on this to do so. This app is what we will use to manage the business when it's ready, not while doing it. I need a fully usable app."*

**Why:** he separates building a system from operating the business. A tool is adopted when finished, not bolted on mid-term. Framing work as urgent pushes him toward an MVP he did not ask for and will not use.

**How to apply:** when he says "app", "webapp", or "dashboard", the deliverable is running software he can open, not a document about software. Skip MVP-phasing language unless he asks for it — spec the complete product and sequence by dependency, not by urgency. Never introduce a deadline he didn't state. Analysis is a means; if two consecutive turns produce no runnable artifact, that's the signal to stop and build.
